## Research-history documents

The repository also retains historical design and audit documents for traceability. They should not be interpreted as superseding the closed v1 evidence summary. When a historical document and the curated v1 summary differ in status or scope, the curated v1 result documents and the v1.0.0 release disposition take precedence.

## Evidence policy

Raw/restricted datasets, model weights, machine-local receipts, runtime state, and full execution reports are intentionally not published. Public result documents are curated summaries derived from the closed evidence package. Post-hoc findings remain diagnostic and do not retroactively alter v1 selection or claims.
