# Code of conduct

AURORA-Ads welcomes technical disagreement and careful scrutiny of research claims. Participation should remain professional, evidence-focused, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas, code, assumptions, and evidence rather than people.
- Be precise about uncertainty and avoid overstating results.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and data-use boundaries.
- Give credit for prior work and distinguish reproduced findings from new findings.
- Keep security-sensitive information out of public threads.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory or degrading remarks, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets/private data, or sustained bad-faith disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when needed to protect contributors, users, or the integrity of the project. Serious security matters should be reported privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
